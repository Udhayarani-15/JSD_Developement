Growth Mindset

A growth mindset is the belief that intelligence, skills, and abilities can be improved through learning, practice, and hard work.
This concept was introduced by psychologist Carol Dweck. People with a growth mindset believe that mistakes are opportunities to learn and become better.
Instead of saying, "I can't do this," they say, "I can't do this yet." This positive way of thinking helps people improve their knowledge and achieve their goals.
